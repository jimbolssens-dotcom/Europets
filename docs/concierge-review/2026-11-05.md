## Fixed and live

- (1) "Staff are in the conversation" now means a real message from staff in the last hour, for both the notice and the AI: the invoice-page link doesn't count, and an exchange the client already answered is over once that answer has waited 30 minutes with no staff reply. A live back-and-forth (staff replying within the hour, the client answering minutes later) still keeps both out, as before.
