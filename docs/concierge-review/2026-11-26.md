## Problems found

1. **Payment screenshot answered, then thanked.** 06729f2c, 25 Nov 10:24: paid online at 10:23:58, sent the screenshot at 10:24:23, got "I've passed it to the team and they'll take a look", then the payment thank-you at 10:25:32. Same in ad22bbb9 (25 Nov 16:32) and d1217d8f (20 Nov, on the last watch list). Should have been: no notice; the thank-you is the answer. Why: a photo is always handed to staff with a message, and the concierge didn't know a payment had just come in.
2. **Greeting reply crossing the question, again.** eec3fe5a, 23 Nov 10:24: "Hi", then 21 seconds later the booking question; "How can we help you today?" went out a second before the question arrived. The 15-second greeting wait from 23 Nov wasn't long enough for a slow typist.
3. **Staff replies bounced (fixed before this review).** 03f4266a, 25 Nov 11:44: the owner of a pet that died wrote from a second phone; the AI answered that phone, but staff's condolence and cremation question went to the number on file and failed (Meta #131047, no conversation in 24 hours). The owner wrote "Don't bother replying". Fixed and live 25 Nov 14:44 (#89): replies now go to the number the client last wrote from.
4. **Invoices and one update not delivered (Meta #131049).** 16 staff sends failed this period with "not delivered to maintain healthy ecosystem engagement", in 10 threads: 15 "Here is your invoice" messages and one hospital update (7bde4346, 25 Nov 17:46, one day after the owner asked someone to explain the PCR results). Why: outside the 24-hour window a staff message goes out as the general `clinic_message` template, and Meta limits how many of those (marketing-type) messages each person receives. The pay-link message that usually follows (its own template) arrived every time, so clients could still pay; the hospital update simply didn't arrive.

## Fixed and live

- (1) A message that is only photos, within 15 minutes of the client paying through a pay link, gets no notice: the automatic thank-you confirms the payment. A photo with words, or one sent later, is handled as before. `lib/paymentScreenshot.js`, `lib/whatsappConcierge.js`.
- (2) A bare greeting now waits 30 seconds (was 15) for the question that follows. `lib/conciergeDebounce.js`.
- Test: `tests/concierge-review-1126.test.mjs` replays both threads.

## Waiting for your OK

- **Payment thank-you for clients with many open invoices** (branch `thank-you-balance-summary`). A rescuer paying small amounts got 8 more thank-yous repeating a six-figure balance that jumps up and down as new invoices are added (346e2ae6). For a client with more than 10 open invoices, the balance line would change:
  - Before: "Account update: A balance of AED [six figures] is still open for [60-odd] open invoices. You can settle it securely here: [link]"
  - After: "Account update: You can see everything still open, and settle any of it, here: [link]"
  Clients with 10 or fewer open invoices see no change.
- **Undelivered invoices and updates (#131049):** two options, your choice:
  - a) An invoice sent outside the 24-hour window skips the general template, since the pay-link message (which does arrive) already carries the link; staff see "sent as pay link" instead of "Not delivered".
  - b) A dedicated "your invoice" template (utility, not marketing), submitted to Meta for approval.
  For hospital updates, the owner's live updates page already has them; a failed send could show staff a "call instead" prompt.
- From before: the "no automated WhatsApp replies" switch per client, booking after a handover, and branches `concierge-book-with-reason` and `concierge-vet-preference`.
