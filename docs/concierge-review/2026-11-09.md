## Problems found

1. **A routine booking was handed to staff because the reason was a symptom.** 280652bc, 6 Nov:
   - At 12:55 the client asked for 4:45 that day. The AI said it was free and asked what the visit was for.
   - The answer, "stomach issue", made it hand over instead of booking.
   - "Book" and "?" (13:14) then got nothing. At 14:04 the client wrote "This AI reply is annoying, I'm coming 4:45", and staff booked it at 14:18.
   - Should have: booked the 4:45 consult with "stomach issue" as the reason.
   - Why: the rules send any described symptom to staff "however minor", with no exception for the reason of a visit the client is already booking. Changing that changes what the AI may do, so it waits for your OK (below).
2. **A client who asked for no automated messages still gets them.** 7bde4346:
   - On 7 Nov at 12:33 the client wrote that he does not consent to automated messages. At 18:13 staff replied that the number would be taken out so none would come.
   - On 8 Nov at 14:04 and 17:23 the AI still answered him.
   - There is no way today to switch the AI off for one client.
   - Should have: no AI replies or notices to him, only staff. Needs your OK (proposal below).
3. **A double reply at night.** 3a7a2070, 6 Nov 22:28:
   - Four quick messages saying the pet was doing well after surgery got an AI reply. Twelve seconds later a notice followed, saying it had been passed to the vet.
   - Why: the fourth message landed while the first answer was being sent, so a second run started. That run saw good news after a surgery and handed it to the vet.
   - Should have: one reply. Seen once (see watch list).
4. **A notice answered an old message.** a07a62c8, 6 Nov 15:40:
   - "Please update me when the doctor checks him" got "I've let the team know you're outside right now. If nobody has come out to you in a minute, please call us".
   - That was the answer to "Am outside" at 15:02, which had been held back then while staff were in the thread.
   - Should have: answered the newest message. Seen once (see watch list).

## Waiting for your OK

- **Book a consult even when the reason is a symptom** (problem 1). Branch `concierge-book-with-reason`.
  - When the client has picked a free consult time and names a problem as the reason ("stomach issue", "he's scratching a lot"), the AI books it with that reason and confirms it.
  - It still hands to staff when it could be urgent (bleeding, breathing trouble, an accident, collapse, not eating or drinking for a day or more, a pet in a lot of pain), or when the client asks what to do or wants advice rather than a visit.
  - Before: "stomach issue" → "I've passed this to our team along with your request for 4:45 PM today, and they'll come back to you as soon as they can."

  - Test: `tests/concierge-book-with-reason.test.mjs`.
