## Problems found

1. **Markdown asterisks in WhatsApp.** 815d8d8e, 20 Nov 09:05: "**Today, Friday 20 Nov** • 10:00 AM …"; 346e2ae6, 20 Nov 16:28: "- **[pet]**, staying in our hospital …". WhatsApp bolds with one asterisk, so the client saw the double asterisks. Why: the AI writes Markdown bold now and then, and nothing turned it into WhatsApp's.
2. **A reply crossing the client's question.** A new number, 22 Nov 19:34: "Good Evening", then 10 seconds later "Can I schedule a check up for three of my cats"; the reply to the greeting ("How can I help you today?") went out half a second after the question arrived. Third time this has been seen (3a7a2070, 68d75e20 earlier). Why: the concierge waits 2 seconds, then the model takes about 8; the "newer message?" check before sending ran a moment before the question was stored.
3. **Left over from before 22 Nov's fixes** (no new action): a rescuer got three payment reminders in two days (21 Nov 10:20, 21 Nov 12:20, 22 Nov 10:20, thread 7f5977b8); "Anything?" (b511c48d, 21 Nov 18:59) and a repeated "605 left or 3155 left?" (282f4eee, 22 Nov 11:25) got no reply. The 10-day payment reminder gap and the reply to a client chasing went live on 22 Nov 15:35 UAE; I'll check next time that they hold.

## Fixed and live

- (1) Every AI reply and notice now goes out in WhatsApp's formatting: **bold** becomes *bold*, a "###" heading becomes a bold line. The words never change. `lib/whatsappFormat.js`.
- (2) A message that is only a greeting ("Hi", "Good evening", "Salam") waits 15 seconds instead of 2 before the concierge answers, so the question that follows is read together with it. Anything longer still waits 2 seconds. `lib/conciergeDebounce.js`, `app/api/whatsapp/webhook/route.js`.
